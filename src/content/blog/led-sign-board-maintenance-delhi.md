A new glow sign board or set of LED letters looks great on the day it goes up. A year later, the same sign can look tired: a dark corner, a flickering letter, a faded face or a layer of dust that dulls the light. In Delhi, the weather is usually the reason.

Most of this can be prevented or fixed early with a little attention. Here is what to look out for through the year.

## What Delhi weather does to a sign

**Summer heat.** Long hours of strong sun fade printed faces, especially on fronts that face south or west. Heat also builds up inside the sign box, which is hard on LED modules and power supplies.

**Dust.** Dust settles on the face and on top of letters. It makes the sign look dull by day and dims the light at night. Near main roads and construction sites, it builds up quickly.

**Monsoon rain.** Water is the main enemy of anything electrical. It gets in through poorly sealed joints, cable entries and drain holes that are blocked. It can also rust MS frames from the inside.

**Winter smog.** Smog leaves a grey film on surfaces. It is not as damaging as rain, but signs need a clean once the season passes.

## Simple checks you can do yourself

You do not need to open the sign to spot most problems. Once a month, look at it carefully, by day and at night.

- **At night:** is the light even across the face? Are there dark patches, flickering sections or letters that are dimmer than others?
- **By day:** is the face faded, torn or loose? Are there rust stains running down from the frame?
- **The wiring:** are cables hanging loose, rubbing against sharp edges or lying in a place where water collects?
- **The power supply:** if you can see it, is it covered and dry?
- **The timer:** is the sign switching on and off at the right time?

If you notice a problem early, the repair is usually small. A dark section left for months can lead to a bigger failure.

## Cleaning, safely

1. **Switch the sign off** and isolate the power before touching it.
2. Use a soft cloth with plain water or a mild soap solution.
3. Avoid harsh chemicals, thinners and abrasive pads. They scratch acrylic and damage printed faces.
4. Do not spray water into the edges or the back of the sign.
5. For high signs, do not use an unsafe ladder. Ask your sign maker to include cleaning with a service visit.

## Before the monsoon

The weeks before the rains are the most useful time for a check-up. Ask your sign maker, or an electrician you trust, to look at:

- **Seals and joints** around the frame and letters, and redo any that have cracked
- **Cable entry points**, so water cannot run along the wire into the sign
- **Drain holes**, which some sign boxes and letters have, so that any water that gets in can get out
- **The power supply**, which should sit in a covered, ventilated spot and not on the floor of a balcony where water pools
- **Rust spots** on MS frames, to be treated and painted before the rain makes them worse

After the monsoon, check again for water marks inside the face, rust and any sections that have stopped working.

## Before the summer

- Check that the sign box has some ventilation. Heat trapped inside shortens the life of the LEDs and the driver.
- Look at the face. If it has faded noticeably, plan a reprint before the peak season for your business.
- Clean off the winter dust and smog.

## Glow sign board or LED letters: different weak points

**Glow sign boards** usually fail at the face (fading, tearing, loosening from the frame) or at individual LED module rows. The good news is that the face can be reprinted and refitted on the same frame, and modules can be replaced in sections.

**LED letters** are more sensitive to water because each letter has its own wiring and joints. A single letter going dark is usually a loose or wet connection. Check the seals on each letter and the cable path between them.

## When to repair and when to replace

Repair makes sense when the frame is sound and only parts have failed: a few modules, a driver, a torn face. Replacement makes sense when the frame is badly rusted, the sign no longer matches your branding, or problems keep coming back in different places.

A good sign maker will tell you honestly which one you need. Ask for the reason, not just the quote.
