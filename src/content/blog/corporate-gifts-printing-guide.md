Every company has a cupboard of gifts nobody wanted: odd-shaped pens, keychains, a third calendar. The goal of a corporate gift is simple. It should get used, and your brand should be on it in a way that still looks good after a hundred washes or a year on a desk.

## Start with who it is for

**Employees**: practical, everyday items they will use at work or at home. T-shirts for events, quality bottles, diaries, laptop bags.

**Dealers and distributors**: items that sit in their shop or office and keep your brand in front of their customers. Wall clocks, desk organisers, branded mugs, calendars.

**Clients**: fewer pieces, better quality. A well-made kit with a personal note works better than many small items.

## Items that usually get used

- **T-shirts and polos**: if the fabric and fit are good
- **Mugs and bottles**: they sit on desks every day
- **Diaries and notebooks**: especially at the start of the year
- **Bags**: laptop bags, totes and backpacks
- **Desk items**: organisers, coasters, wireless chargers

What these have in common is quality. A cheap T-shirt will be worn once. A good one will be worn for years.

## Match the printing method to the item

| Item | Common methods |
|---|---|
| T-shirts (bulk, simple design) | Screen printing |
| T-shirts (small runs, many colours) | DTF or heat transfer |
| Polos, caps, jackets | Embroidery |
| White or coated mugs | Sublimation |
| Metal bottles, pens | Laser engraving or pad printing |
| Diaries | Foil stamping, debossing or UV printing |
| Bags | Screen printing or embroidery |

The right method depends on the material, the number of colours in your logo and the quantity. Ask for a sample of the exact method before the full order.

## Planning a bulk order

1. **Fix the budget per person** first, then choose items to fit it.
2. **Get samples** of the blank item and one printed sample.
3. **Share the final logo** in vector format (CDR, AI or PDF).
4. **Confirm sizes** for apparel: collect a size list early, not on the last day.
5. **Plan packing**: individual boxes, a printed sleeve, a thank-you card.
6. **Leave buffer time**, especially before Diwali and New Year, when demand peaks.

## Make it a kit

A simple kit (say a diary, a pen, a bottle and a card in a printed box) often feels more thoughtful than one expensive item. When one team handles the box, the card and the branding on every item, the whole kit shares one look.
